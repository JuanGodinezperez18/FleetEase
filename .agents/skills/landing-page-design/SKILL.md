# FleetEase Landing Page

The landing page is not the dashboard. Its job is to communicate the product and drive a primary action.

FleetEase should feel like a serious Mexican fleet-operations product built by people who understand rental and driver-vehicle operations. Avoid generic AI SaaS language and stock-template composition.

Before editing, inspect the current landing page, logo, typography, color system, screenshots/assets and actual product capabilities. Never invent features.

Use only sections justified by the offer and audience. Possible sections include value proposition, operational pain, product workflow, high-value capabilities, proof/credibility, focused CTA and FAQ/trust information. Do not add sections merely to make the page longer.

Use concrete operational outcomes. Prefer accurate terms such as fleet control, rentas, gastos, pagos, mantenimiento, socios and profitability. Avoid unsupported claims or fabricated customer numbers.

Prefer real FleetEase product UI/screenshots over stock illustrations. Maintain intentional typography, spacing, surfaces and motion. Avoid excessive gradients, floating-card soup and decorative blobs.

Keep one primary CTA visually dominant. Design mobile and tablet intentionally. Maintain semantic headings, metadata, alt text, keyboard access, contrast and performance.
