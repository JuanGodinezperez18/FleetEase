# Anti-Slop Landing Audit 001
**Date:** 2026-10-23  
**Scope:** Public FleetEase landing page, inspected from `master`.  
**Mode:** `after`. This report contains findings only. No application source files were modified.  
**Brand constraints retained:** official logo component (`BrandLogo` using `/logo.png`), graphite `#080a0f`, lime `#d7ff3f`.

## Findings

### 1. HIGH | Illustrative dashboard exposes realistic financial and fleet figures
- **Rule:** R-17 (Data & Numbers), R-38 (Real Content or Honest Placeholder).
- **Evidence:** `src/components/landing/hero-dashboard.tsx:41-43, 98, 108` renders `24` vehicles, `$96.4K` income, `$31.8K` costs, `67%` margin, `22 / 24 activas`, and `3 mantenimientos`. The component says the dashboard is illustrative, but the numbers themselves resemble live business data.
- **Reason:** Anti-Slop R-17 says not to display unsourced numbers when real data is unavailable. A general “illustrative” label may not be sufficient to prevent visitors from interpreting the values as product evidence.
- **Suggested correction (not applied):** Replace the realistic figures with clearly non-numeric illustrative visual elements, or make the example unmistakably synthetic in the visual itself and validate that against R-17 before implementation.

### 2. HIGH | Landing copy contains a forbidden em dash
- **Rule:** R-02 (Copywriting).
- **Evidence:** `src/app/page.tsx:115`: “...no estaban en el presupuesto — antes de que...”.
- **Reason:** R-02 explicitly forbids the em dash character in UI copy written under these rules.
- **Suggested correction (not applied):** Replace it with a comma, period, colon, or parentheses without changing the meaning.

### 3. HIGH | Mobile navigation toggle is likely below the 44 px target
- **Rule:** R-03 (Mobile Responsiveness).
- **Evidence:** `src/components/landing/landing-header.tsx:54` uses `p-2` around a standard 24 px Lucide icon. That produces an approximately 40 px square target before any additional styles.
- **Reason:** R-03 requires interactive targets to meet at least 44 px and calls out mobile navigation usability. This is a source inspection finding; the rendered target has not been measured in a browser.
- **Suggested correction (not applied):** Give the button explicit minimum width and height of 44 px or larger, then verify at mobile breakpoints.

### 4. HIGH | Small muted text may fail WCAG AA contrast
- **Rule:** R-25 (Color Contrast).
- **Evidence:** Landing content uses classes such as `text-white/30` and `text-white/35` on the dark `#080a0f` surface; `src/app/fleetease-ui.css` explicitly maps those classes to low-opacity white.
- **Reason:** These opacity levels are likely below the 4.5:1 minimum for normal-size text. This needs a contrast measurement on the final rendered combinations before it can be marked confirmed.
- **Suggested correction (not applied):** Raise contrast for meaningful text and metadata, or remove nonessential low-contrast text. Preserve the lime accent and overall dark brand direction.

### 5. HIGH | Enterprise plan promises capabilities that need current product verification
- **Rule:** R-38 (Real Content or Honest Placeholder), R-36 (No Fabricated Claims).
- **Evidence:** `src/components/landing/landing-pricing.tsx:34-36` claims “Vehículos y usuarios ilimitados” and “Acompañamiento empresarial”.
- **Reason:** These are concrete commercial promises. The audit did not find evidence in the landing source that validates their availability in the current Enterprise offering. They should not be marketed as live capabilities unless product owners verify them.
- **Suggested correction (not applied):** Confirm each promise against the current Enterprise implementation and terms. Remove or clearly label unavailable capabilities until verified; do not infer that Enterprise is complete from the plan card.

### 6. MEDIUM | Decorative grid and multiple glow treatments risk stacking effects without a documented visual purpose
- **Rule:** R-07 (Background), R-01 (Color & Gradients), Core Principle (purpose test).
- **Evidence:** `src/app/page.tsx:72-79` layers a full-screen square grid, a radial hero glow, and a dark branded surface; `src/components/landing/hero-dashboard.tsx:12-21` adds another radial glow and blur treatment.
- **Reason:** The established graphite/lime palette is intentional and should remain. The grid and repeated glow effects, however, resemble patterns explicitly listed as warning signs. The source comments explain paint/LCP considerations, but do not state the design purpose of the visual treatments.
- **Suggested correction (not applied):** Keep only effects that strengthen the fleet-management identity or establish hierarchy. Do not remove brand colors or redesign the visual direction as part of this finding without approval.

## Verification limits
- Reviewed the current source files and the repository's `DESIGN.md`, `AGENTS.md`, and `antislop.md`.
- This is a source-level audit, not a browser-run audit. No production URL, rendered viewport, keyboard walkthrough, build, or automated contrast tool was executed as part of this report.
- No source files, business logic, routes, assets, or deployment settings were changed.
- Only the numbered findings explicitly approved by the owner should be fixed. Findings not approved remain untouched.
